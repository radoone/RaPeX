Why we built the app

A dangerous product may have a different name or packaging in your store. Manual comparisons take time.

The app finds possible matches and gathers evidence in one place in Shopify Admin.

We email you immediately about new possible matches. When there are no new findings, a weekly update brings peace of mind.

Our principles

Official data

You decide

We show possible matches and evidence. We do not automatically unpublish products.

Traceable decisions

An independent project

We are not affiliated with or endorsed by the European Commission, Safety Gate or Shopify. Possible matches support review; they are not legal assessments or legal advice.
