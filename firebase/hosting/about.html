Why we built Safety Gate Monitor

A recalled product may appear under a different name, brand, package, or supplier listing. That makes manual checks slow and easy to miss—especially as a catalog grows.

We built Safety Gate Monitor to continuously compare Shopify catalogs with the EU Safety Gate system, surface likely matches, and give merchants a practical place to review and document each decision.

The goal is not to replace professional judgement. It is to make reliable evidence easier to find, the next action easier to take, and the quiet periods genuinely reassuring.

Three principles guide the product.

Official data first

Monitoring is grounded in product-safety alerts published through the European Commission’s Safety Gate system.

The merchant stays in control

The app highlights potential matches and supporting evidence. It does not automatically unpublish products or make the final decision for you.

Decisions should be traceable

Reviews, resolutions, and dismissals are recorded so your team can understand what was checked and what happened next.

What Safety Gate Monitor is—and what it is not.

Safety Gate Monitor is independent software for Shopify merchants. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the European Commission, EU Safety Gate, or Shopify. Potential matches are decision support, not a legal determination or legal advice.
