Regulatory Context

Official alerts help you decide what to review.

The EU Safety Gate system shares alerts about dangerous non-food products. The General Product Safety Regulation applies to businesses selling consumer products in the EU.

A Safety Gate alert can be a useful signal for a merchant to compare product identifiers, photos and batches. Requirements and corrective steps depend on the product and the country.

Safety Gate Monitor helps organize possible matches and review decisions. It does not make a legal determination or change product status for you.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the EU Safety Gate (formerly RAPEX)?

EU Safety Gate is the European Union's rapid alert system for dangerous non-food consumer products. It alerts member states and consumers about hazardous toys, electronics, cosmetics, and equipment subject to recalls.

Why is this more than a one-off AI search?

Products can be renamed or photographed differently, and new Safety Gate alerts are published over time. The app combines catalog comparison, ongoing updates, a review queue and a record of merchant decisions.

Does this app modify or unpublish my Shopify products automatically?

No. Safety Gate Monitor is strictly advisory and operates under read-only catalog access. It flags potential risks and creates actionable review alerts in your Shopify Admin, leaving all publishing decisions entirely under your control.

What is included in the free scan?

We check up to 100 publicly accessible Shopify products against Safety Gate alerts and email a summary of possible matches. It does not cover private listings and is not a certificate of safety.
