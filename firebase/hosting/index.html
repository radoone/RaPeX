Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about safety, free scanning & GPSR compliance.

How many items are in the Safety Gate database and how many are added monthly?

The official EU Safety Gate (RAPEX) database currently holds over 35,000 dangerous non-food consumer products. The European Commission releases new reports every Friday, adding approximately 200 to 300+ new alerts and mandatory product recalls every month (roughly 50 to 80 per week).

What specific penalties and legal obligations apply under GPSR and market authorities?

Under EU Regulation 2023/988 (GPSR) and consumer protection laws: (1) Merchants must immediately cease sales and execute a mandatory product recall, notifying all affected buyers. (2) Merchants must provide a full refund or remedy without delay (even without original packaging/receipts). (3) Market surveillance authorities can impose fines ranging from €66,400 up to €332,000 or significant percentages of annual turnover for serious hazards.

How does the free store scan work?

Simply enter your Shopify store domain and email address. The app performs a full scan of your catalog against the official EU Safety Gate (RAPEX) database and sends the report directly to your inbox.

What is the EU Safety Gate (formerly RAPEX) system?

EU Safety Gate (formerly RAPEX) is the European Union's rapid alert system for dangerous non-food consumer products. The European Commission publishes weekly reports on recalled items (toys, electronics, cosmetics, chargers, apparel) that pose serious health or safety risks. You can browse the database at ec.europa.eu/safety-gate-alerts.

What is the GPSR regulation and why does it affect Shopify merchants?

The EU General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR - EU 2023/988, effective Dec 13, 2024) applies to all e-commerce stores selling to EU consumers. It mandates active product safety verification, responsible EU economic operator identification, and instant cooperation with surveillance authorities.

How does Visual AI detect products if suppliers changed names or branding?

Suppliers often relabel or white-label products under different names. Safety Gate Monitor uses advanced visual AI to inspect both product descriptions and actual product photos, spotting visually identical dangerous goods regardless of name changes.
