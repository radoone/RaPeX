What happens during the scan and how does matching work?
  • Public products only
  • Possible matches to review
  • Your products stay unchanged
  1. After you confirm the single-use email link (valid for 30 minutes), we read only your store's publicly available products (titles, brands, models, categories and photos).
  2. We compare product text and photos against 35,000+ official EU Safety Gate (RAPEX) records using multimodal vector and visual similarity — spotting identical products even under a different name or language.
  3. Within a few minutes you receive an email with the number of checked products, photos and official case numbers of possible matches, plus a private CSV download link.

A different name. A familiar product.

Suppliers change titles. Listings lose model numbers. ZeroRecall uses AI to compare product details and photos with official EU Safety Gate records, helping you spot possible matches that an exact text search can miss.

EAN first. More evidence when it is missing.

An exact EAN / GTIN match is the strongest signal of product identity. When identifiers are incomplete, AI weighs the brand, model, description and distinctive visual details together. A shared category alone is not enough.

Photos add context that a title cannot.

Similar construction, packaging or visible model markings can help connect differently named listings. Conflicting identifiers and missing evidence matter too: an uncertain match stays a question for review.

Regulatory Context

Product safety in the EU

Safety Gate publishes alerts about dangerous non-food products. The General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR) covers consumer products sold in the EU.

The app provides evidence for review. It does not make legal assessments or change your products.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Safety Gate?

The EU alert system for dangerous non-food products, formerly RAPEX. It covers products such as toys, electronics and cosmetics.

Why ongoing checks?

New alerts appear every week. After installation, we compare them with your products.

Does the app change my products?

No. It shows possible matches and evidence in Shopify Admin. You decide what to do.

What is included in the free scan?

All products available in your store’s public Shopify catalog and an emailed summary of possible matches. Private listings are excluded; the scan is not a safety certificate.

What if no possible matches are found?

Your report will show the number of checked products and that no possible matches were found in this check. This does not certify product safety. New alerts can appear later.
