1. Install ZeroRecall on a Shopify development or review store with sample non-food products. Open it from Shopify Admin > Apps > ZeroRecall. Shopify sign-in is sufficient.
2. Start the initial catalog check from the dashboard and wait for completion. Catalog Coverage shows current, changed, and unchecked product versions. To test an individual product, open Catalog Coverage, find a sample product, and use its check action.
3. To exercise the review flow, use a draft fixture with identifiers and details from a real EU Safety Gate alert. A fresh catalog without matching products can legitimately have an empty Review Queue. In the prepared RAPEX Beta 01 demo, the Wall Rallyist N9920C — TEST fixture already has a possible match.
4. In Review Queue, choose Compare products. Compare the catalog details with the source photos, identifiers, and risk information, and open the official Safety Gate record. On a disposable fixture, record a decision and confirm it appears in Decision history. The dashboard links to the Checks and decisions report.
5. To test monitoring entitlement on the authorized rapex-beta-01 store, open pricing through Settings > Subscription & Safety Monitoring or the dashboard, select Review test, and confirm the amount is Free. After Shopify returns to ZeroRecall, check Settings for ZeroRecall Pro (Active). This private plan is restricted to that store. Subscription activation and completed monitoring activity are shown separately.
6. In Monitoring Settings, review the email options and language selector. Use only a test inbox if enabling email. Possible matches require merchant verification. The app does not certify safety or change the product's status in Shopify.
