Regulatory Context

Why EU Safety Gate and GPSR matter for every merchant.

Under the EU General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR – Regulation EU 2023/988), online sellers are legally responsible for ensuring products sold to EU consumers are safe and not subject to active safety recalls.

National market surveillance authorities (such as SOI in Slovakia, ČOI in Czechia, BAuA in Germany, and DGCCRF in France) actively inspect online shops. Selling recalled goods can trigger mandatory recalls, customer refunds without receipt, payment processor holds, and fines up to €100,000+.

Safety Gate Monitor operates inside your Shopify Admin to give you proactive proof of due diligence and an immutable audit trail ready for any authority inspection.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the EU Safety Gate (formerly RAPEX)?

EU Safety Gate is the European Union's rapid alert system for dangerous non-food consumer products. It alerts member states and consumers about hazardous toys, electronics, cosmetics, and equipment subject to recalls.

How does visual AI detect rebranded or white-label products?

Suppliers often rename or repackage products. Our system uses multimodal vector embeddings to compare both text specifications and actual product photographs against recall photos in the EU database, catching rebadged products even when titles differ.

Does this app modify or unpublish my Shopify products automatically?

No. Safety Gate Monitor is strictly advisory and operates under read-only catalog access. It flags potential risks and creates actionable review alerts in your Shopify Admin, leaving all publishing decisions entirely under your control.

What is included in the free scan?

The free scan audits your current public catalog against the entire EU Safety Gate database and sends a comprehensive safety summary report to your email, highlighting any products that require your attention.
