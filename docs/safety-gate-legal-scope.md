# Safety Gate history and the legal basis

Primary-source review on 9 November 2026: [GPSR, Regulation (EU) 2023/988, consolidated 29 May 2026](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02023R0988-20260529) and [DSA, Regulation (EU) 2022/2065](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj/eng). Official English OJ PDFs were downloaded directly from the Publications Office using content negotiation and retained with extracted text and hashes in `outputs/gpsr-research-2026-11-09/`.

The reviewed provisions do not prescribe an exhaustive historical Safety Gate comparison for every product or require a full historical rescan on every run:

- GPSR Article 5 permits only safe products to be placed or made available on the market. Article 12 sets distributor checks, restrictions on making suspected non-compliant products available, and corrective/reporting duties. Article 14 requires internal product-safety processes. These are substantive duties; they do not prescribe an AI scanner, top-k budget or historical time window.
- GPSR Article 22(6) requires online marketplaces to take into account regular dangerous-product information received through Safety Gate for their relevant voluntary detection/removal measures. Article 22(7) requires use of at least Safety Gate for the product-safety aspect of DSA Article 31(3).
- DSA Article 31(3) requires the relevant online platforms to make reasonable efforts to **randomly check** official, freely accessible, machine-readable databases/interfaces for products/services identified as illegal. It does not say to compare every offer against every historical notification. Scope and exemptions still matter; an ordinary own-catalog merchant is not automatically the operator of an intermediary marketplace for third-party traders.

Do not market “EU law requires every product to be AI-scanned against every historical Safety Gate record” as an express statutory rule. Equally, there is no general permission here to disregard an applicable dangerous-product notice because it is old. Absence of a Safety Gate match does not certify product safety or fulfil every GPSR obligation. Product-specific legislation and the operator's actual role may add requirements.

The engineering recommendation is to search the current index across all available history for a new/changed product, persist completed results, and process new **and corrected** source records through journal deltas. Reuse embeddings and unchanged completed pair results. This is a technical risk-control/cost choice, not a statutory certification that this workflow alone establishes compliance. Searching an all-history index with a bounded shortlist does not mean every historical pair received AI analysis.

Publications Office metadata identifies Regulation (EU) 2024/2748 and a 29 May 2026 GPSR consolidation. After direct consolidated-PDF retrieval failed, the corresponding EUR-Lex text was retrieved through the Jina reader and the relevant articles checked there too. The consolidation adds emergency provisions; the reviewed Articles 5, 12, 14, 22 and 34 retain the provisions described above. This note records those provisions, rather than an exhaustive legal opinion across all applicable product-specific laws.
